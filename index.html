Buggy

Buggy the Clown tried to scare a town by threatening them with his “fearsome pirate skills,” but tripped over his own detachable nose and rolled straight into a barrel of fish. He popped out, covered in slime, screaming, “Fear me, or I’ll… uh… chase you later!” By the end, the townspeople were laughing so hard they gave him a parade—while he proudly waved, completely forgetting he was supposed to be terrifying.

About Buggy the Clown

Buggy the Clown is a comical yet unpredictable pirate in the One Piece universe. Famous for his detachable body parts thanks to the Chop-Chop Fruit, he often gets into hilarious situations while trying to appear intimidating.
